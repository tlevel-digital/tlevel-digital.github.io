# T-Level Digital

Open teaching resources for T-Level Digital students and staff. Everything here is written in Markdown, kept in a public repository, and free to share and adapt under the CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 licence.

## Qualifications

[Digital Support and Security](dss/index.md)
:   Guides, practicals, and assignment support for the T-Level in Digital Support and Security, covering the Route Core and the Occupational Specialism. This is the former DSS Wiki.

## Contributing

Resources are welcome from staff and students at any provider. See the [contribution guidance](dss/contribute.md) for how to format a page, or open a pull request on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/tlevel-digital/tlevel-digital.github.io).
