# Employers

As part of the T-Level in Digital Support and Security, students must complete a work experience placement over the course of their qualification to give them the opportunity to apply the skills and theory that they have developed in the classroom. 

Prior to being placed, students are given a good technical grounding and then assessed for their readiness for placement. As an employer, you are given the opportunity to interview students that have been selected as appropriate for your business' needs, this is organised by our industry liaison officer who will provide you with each interviewee's CV prior to the interview and take feedback on completion of the student that you wish to select.
