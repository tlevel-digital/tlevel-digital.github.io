# Welcome to the DSS Wiki

## Home of T-Level Digital Support and Security Resources

Over time I have been creating and collating resources of interest for my T-Level Digital Support Services students and posting them as a bunch of markdown files on [Codeberg](https://codeberg.org/rdmillen/computingeducation), however I've now outgrown this platform and navigation of a stack of markdown files can be a bit of a pain.  As of March 2025, these resources now reside here on The DSS WIki!
